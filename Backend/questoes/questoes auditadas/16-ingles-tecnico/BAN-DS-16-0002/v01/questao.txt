A status page shows the latest information about a service interruption. Readers who want automatic updates should subscribe to email notifications. Otherwise, they will need to revisit the page to check for changes. The emails repeat the information on the page; they do not provide an earlier estimate of when the service will return. Readers can cancel the subscription at any time.

(Texto autoral.)

C) assim que se inscreverem para receber as notificações por email.
