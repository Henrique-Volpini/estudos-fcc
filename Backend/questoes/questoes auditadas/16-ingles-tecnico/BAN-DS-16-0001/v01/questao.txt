The service desk replaced a lengthy installation guide with a checklist. During the first week, new employees completed the routine steps more quickly, yet the number of requests for help remained unchanged. Most requests concerned messages that the checklist did not explain. Rather than bring back the full guide, the team kept the checklist and added a separate section on those messages. It will review next month's support requests to find out whether the addition makes employees less dependent on assistance.

(Texto autoral.)
